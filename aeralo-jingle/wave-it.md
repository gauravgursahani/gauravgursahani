# Aeralo — "Wave It" (the Signal Dance)

**Insight:** in a new country, everyone does the same silly dance to get online. They hold the phone up high, lean out of windows, stand on chairs, wander around hotel lobbies and ask a stranger for the Wi-Fi password. It's universal, a little embarrassing, and funny.
**Idea:** turn that dance into a loopy, nasal earworm. Aeralo is how you stop dancing.
**Tagline:** **Wherever you go, Aeralo.**
**Pronunciation:** **AIR-a-lo**

---

## The jingle

**Verse 1: the signal dance**
```
Wave it high, wave it low,
Wave it left, wave it slow,
Up a chair, by the window —
One bar… two bars… oh no!
```

**Verse 2: the Wi-Fi hunt**
```
Café, lobby, to and fro,
"What's the password?" — "Don't know."
Tiptoe, tiptoe, round you go —
One bar… two bars… oh no!
```

**Tag**
```
(beat of silence)
Wherever you go… Aeralo.
```

**Delivery:**
- **Nasal, deadpan and sing-song** throughout, like a playground taunt. The same voice in every ad.
- **"One bar… two bars… oh no!"** is the recurring hook. It works like "hello, honey bunny", and people should end up chanting it whenever their own signal drops.
- **Every line ends on an "-o" sound**, so "Aeralo" lands as the natural end of the rhyme.
- **Before the tag, the music stops** for a beat. Then the tag comes in calm and clean.

## Why it works
- **Memorability:** a short loop, rhyming "-o" endings, a nasal hook and a repeated "oh no!".
- **Meaning:** the brand's whole message is one image, with no explaining needed. Aeralo means *stop dancing*.
- **Works everywhere:** the dance needs no translation. Only the lyrics and the tagline get adapted.
- **Easy to copy:** the "signal dance" is a ready-made challenge for people to film themselves doing.
