# Aeralo — Insight Brainstorm

## The lead insight (from the user): childlike wonder

**The tension:** in a new place you want to wander like a kid, curious and reckless. But the adult in you keeps checking: *Where am I? How do I get back? What if I'm lost?*
**Sharpened:** *Travel makes us children again, until fear makes us adults.* Reliable data in your pocket is the grown-up safety net that lets the kid in you off the leash.
**Aeralo's role:** to be the safety net, not the destination. You stop thinking about where you are and just roam.

**Ways to say it:**
- "Get lost. You can always be found."
- "Wander like a kid. Aeralo's got the grown-up stuff."
- "The safest way to get lost."
- "Roam free." *(This takes the word "roam" back from roaming charges: it means wonder again, not a bill.)*

**Why it fits a jingle:**
- Both reference jingles are childish at heart: "pumpkin, pumpkin" is baby talk, and Close-Up's sing-song is a playground taunt. A nursery-rhyme or playground-chant form isn't a style choice here. It is the insight.
- Games kids play are all about roaming and getting lost: hide-and-seek, "Ready or not, here I come", follow-the-leader, "Are we there yet?"

## Other insights

**Arriving**
1. The first thing you do after landing isn't look out of the window; it's turn off airplane mode.
2. "Landed." is the universal first text.
3. **The notification flood:** connecting brings a burst of buzzing, the sound of reconnecting to your life.
4. Your first 30 minutes in a new country need data most, and that's when you don't have it.

**Hunting for the internet**
5. Abroad, you judge cafés by their Wi-Fi, not their coffee.
6. "What's the Wi-Fi password?" is the traveller's most-asked question.
7. You photograph the Wi-Fi password.
8. Hotel Wi-Fi works in the lobby but never in your room.
9. Free airport Wi-Fi means 30 minutes, an email form, a survey, then it drops.

**Rationing and fear**
10. **You ration data like water in a desert**, and become a stingier version of yourself.
11. The roaming bill is the trip's last souvenir.
12. The SIM-kiosk ritual, and the home SIM you lose.
13. **The screenshot habit:** screenshotting maps before leaving the Wi-Fi.

**Dependence and identity**
14. Your phone is your passport now.
15. **The hotspot friend:** "Can I hotspot off you?"
16. Your bank texts its security codes to your home number.
17. Offline, you feel like a tourist. Online, you feel like a local.
