# Aeralo — "Landed" Spot Scripts

**Concept:** the landing ritual set to music. The wheels touch down and the phones ping awake. A nasal voice dares the passenger who has no signal. Then the whole cabin sings the "Tokyo… Aeralo" loop.
**Pronunciation:** **AIR-a-lo**, with the stress on the first syllable.
**Voices:**
- **The Nasal Voice:** the Close-Up-style sung voiceover. It must be the same voice in every ad.
- **The Cabin:** the passengers, from many countries, singing the loop.

## Lyric sheet (sung parts only)

```
(nasal, solo)   Do you Aeralo…
                …or do you roam and owe?

(call)          Tokyo, Tokyo —
(response)      Aeralo!
(call)          Mexico, Mexico —
(response)      Aeralo!
(call)          Monaco, Monaco —
(response)      Aeralo!
(call)          Paris-o, Paris-o —
(response)      …Aeralo!

(soft)          …hello, Aeralo.

(all)           Wherever you go…
(nasal)         …Aaair-a-looo.
```

## Production notes
- **Beat:** build it from real phone pings and notification sounds at about 100–110 BPM, so the sound design and the music are the same thing.
- **The pause matters:** leave a half-beat of silence before each "Aeralo!", the way "hello, honey bunny" lands.
- **Casting:** the cabin should look like a real international flight. The joke works because strangers who don't share a language all know the same chorus.
- **Local markets:** swap the captain's city ("Welcome to Mexico City", "Welcome to Lisbon") and put that city first in the loop. Translate only the tagline.
- **Placeholders:** `[X] countries` should be filled in with Aeralo's real coverage number.
